To what extent did Latin influence syntax?

Since high-level programming languages are made to increasingly resemble English, we can now ask what aspects of Latin specifically influence syntax. Since the influence of Latin on English is primarily lexical, it is reasonable to assume its influence on english-like syntax is lexical as well. This section covers the different ways Latin permeates the syntax of modern languages - some of which were even used to build this website!
